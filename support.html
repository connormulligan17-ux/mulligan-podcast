FAQs

How is my podcast delivered?

Your episode arrives as an MP3 attachment in your inbox, along with your personal RSS feed URL. Paste that URL into Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or any podcast app to subscribe and get new episodes automatically.

How long until my first episode?

Your first episode ships the same day you subscribe. You'll get an email with the MP3 and your feed URL as soon as it's ready.

What Instagram data do you use?

We analyze your bio, posts, captions, hashtags, and engagement patterns — all publicly available. No login or password is ever required.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime with no penalties. Your feed stays active and you'll always have access to past episodes.

Can I upgrade or change plans?

Yes. Message us at the address below and we'll update your subscription. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Is the audio really hands-off?

Completely. We handle the analysis, script writing, and narration. You receive a finished MP3 — no reviewing, no editing, no coordination needed.
