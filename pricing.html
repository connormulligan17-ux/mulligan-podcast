Questions before you sign up

What exactly do I get each week?
A finished MP3 (8–12 minutes), episode show notes, and a content recommendation. Grow and Scale plans also include a weekly growth metric update.
How does the first episode get made?
After signup, we send a 5-question form about your niche, audience, and goals. You fill it out, we start immediately. Launch ships in 7 days, Grow in 5, Scale in 3.
Do I have to approve scripts before they record?
No. It's fully automated — we don't send scripts for approval. If something in the audio doesn't land right, you tell us and we adjust for next time.
What does "your own RSS feed" mean?
Your podcast gets a feed URL on your own domain (e.g., podcast.yourdomain.com). Listeners subscribe through Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or any app. Permanently yours — and keep every episode.
