The Product

Your Instagram. A real podcast. Every week.

CreatorPod builds a weekly audio show from your Instagram content — narrated by AI, delivered to a private feed on your own domain, subscribed to through Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or any app.

Your Instagram already has an audience. The problem is reach — every post disappears in hours, and there is no strategy layer on top of your content. CreatorPod turns your account into a weekly audio show that gives your followers a reason to come back, and gives you a strategy for what to post next — narrated by Emma, delivered every week, with zero work from you.
