The Process

From Instagram URL to Apple Podcasts listing

1

Paste your Instagram URL

We pull your posts, captions, hashtags, follower data, and content themes — publicly, no login required.

2

We benchmark your niche

Your content is compared against top performers. We find what's working, what's missing, and what your audience wants that you're not giving them.

3

Emma writes and narrates your episode

A custom script around your growth opportunities. Emma — our AI voice — narrates it tuned to your niche. You get an MP3, ready to publish.

4

Published to your own RSS feed

A private feed on your own domain (e.g., podcast.yourdomain.com). Listeners subscribe through Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or any app. Permanently yours.

5

Repeat every week

New episode, same day each week. Each builds on the last — tracking performance, evolving strategy, growing your audience through a medium with 3x the engagement of feed posts.
